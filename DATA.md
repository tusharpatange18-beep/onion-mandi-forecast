# Data: licence, credits and what this repository contains

## The dataset

> Shelar, R., Hajare, A., Mangarule, S. *Preprocessed onion 2014-2024 prices, weather and events* (Version 1). Mendeley Data, 2025.
> DOI: [10.17632/ds9jmxp9zy.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/ds9jmxp9zy.1). Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.

Upstream sources named by the dataset authors:

- **Agmarknet**, Directorate of Marketing and Inspection (DMI), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India:
  daily wholesale prices (min, max, modal) and arrivals by market and variety.
- **India Meteorological Department (IMD)** gridded rainfall and temperature data (accessed by the authors through the IMDLIB package).
  This project does not use the weather columns in version 1.
- Festival and event calendars curated by the dataset authors.

## What this repository contains, and what it does not

- **Not included:** the raw file, the cleaned panel, and every row-level derived file (backtest forecasts, serving forecasts, the anomaly
  list, run directories). They are listed in `.gitignore`. To reproduce, download the dataset from the DOI above (see README, *Reproduce*).
- **Included:** code; aggregate tables in `results/*.csv` (metrics, coverage, counts, summary statistics); figures in `results/figures/`
  derived from the data; reports in `docs/`. These are derived works under CC BY 4.0 and carry the attribution above.
- **Synthetic test data only:** `tests/fixtures/forecasts_fixture.parquet` is generated by `tests/fixtures/make_fixture_forecasts.py`;
  it contains made-up numbers for testing the API container and is not derived from the dataset.

## Changes made to the data (as CC BY 4.0 asks)

Five Maharashtra markets were selected; one variety per market (its most frequent) is used; days are placed on a daily calendar grid with
missing days kept missing; six reviewed outlier quotes were set to missing (originals kept in the cleaned panel); stale runs and other
outliers are only flagged. Every rule and the number of rows it touched is in `docs/cleaning_log.md`.

## Event dates

Export-policy and COVID dates used to label periods were taken from public news and government sources listed with their confidence in
`docs/eda_findings.md`. They are context for description, not part of the dataset.
